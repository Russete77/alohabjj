  _fonte: https://jitsmagazine.com/mica-galvao-becomes-second-person-ever-to-win-super-grand-slam/_

- [nao_confirmado] Após a derrota para Galvão, PJ Barch venceu a luta pelo bronze contra Elijah Dorsey via chave de calcanhar (inside heel hook) em 9:44.  
  _fonte: https://www.mmamania.com/2024/8/18/24223253/adcc-2024-day-2-results-and-highlights-duarte-subs-lovato-diogo-beats-diego-in-tournament-finals_
